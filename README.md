## 🎨 Design Concept

The website is built around the concept of **water as a representation of adaptability and continuous flow**, combined with modern technology aesthetics.

The visual system uses:

* Transparent glass surfaces
* Liquid-inspired gradients
* Cyan and blue highlights
* Soft glow effects
* Floating droplets
* Ripple animations
* Minimal futuristic elements

The goal is to maintain a balance between **visual creativity and professional developer presentation**.
